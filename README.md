# Nova Fork

A simple video player for Android in the spirit of VLC, with a poster and backdrop library.

- **Plays almost anything** with libVLC: MKV, MP4, AVI, HEVC, AV1, DTS/TrueHD audio, ASS/SRT subtitles.
- **Posters and backdrops** from TMDB, with poster.jpg / fanart.jpg next to your files as a fallback.
- **Sources**: device storage, folders you pick (including SD cards and USB drives), SMB shares on your
  network, and stream URLs (HTTP, HLS, RTSP…).
- **Phone and tablet layouts**: a phone gets a compact bottom-bar app; tablets get a navigation rail,
  cinematic heroes and a two-pane library.

See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for how it is put together.
